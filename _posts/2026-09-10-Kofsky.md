# Join us for a conversation with Dr. Janice Kofsky

On Tuesday, September 15, the Bioinformatics Assembly of Students (BiAS) hosts Dr. Janice Kofsky for an evening conversation about careers in bioinformatics. Dr. Kofsky is a UNC Charlotte alumna and a Plant Genomics and Quantitative Genetics Data Scientist at Confluence Genetics. She will talk about her role and about the broader job market for bioinformaticians.

- When: Tuesday, September 15, 2026, from 5:30 to 6:30 PM
- Where: Bioinformatics Building, Room 105, UNC Charlotte
- Who: all students interested in a bioinformatics career

Please join us, and bring your questions. Dr. Kofsky went from UNC Charlotte to an industry data science job, and she will share what that path looks like from the inside.

## Thank you

We thank BiAS and [Carmalita Govan](https://cci.charlotte.edu/directory/carmalita-govan/), Director of Professional Education for Graduate Studies in the Office of the Dean, for this initiative. Seminars like this one connect our students with alumni who already do the work our students are training for.

See you on Tuesday.
