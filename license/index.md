# License and how to cite

## Posts

I wrote the posts on this site unless a post names another author. I reserve all rights to my posts.

You may do these things without asking:

- Read any post, link to it, and share the link.
- Quote short passages in your own work, as long as you cite the post.

You need my permission to republish a post in full or in large part, in any language. That includes other websites, blogs, newsletters, social media, and printed collections.

You may never use a post commercially, sell it, or use it to promote a product or service. You may never place a post behind a paywall or a subscription.

- Posts by other authors belong to those authors.

- The code that runs this site comes from the Greene Lab's lab website template and is under the BSD 3-Clause License, in the site's [GitHub repository](https://github.com/machadodj/machadodj.github.io).

## How to cite

Each post ends with a citation you can copy. Posts follow this format:

Machado, D. J. (2026, November 24). A letter to families of university students who are struggling. Phyloinformatics Lab. https://phyloinformatics.com/2026/11/24/StrugglingStudents.html

Cite another page of the site like this:

Phyloinformatics Lab. (n.d.). Research. https://phyloinformatics.com/research/

## How to ask

Write to me through the [contact page](/contact/). Tell me which post you want to republish, where it will appear, and who will read it.
