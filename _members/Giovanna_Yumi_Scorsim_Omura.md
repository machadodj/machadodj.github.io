# Giovanna Yumi Scorsim Omura

## Who am I?

Hello!

I am a malacologist, and I study land snails. I defended my Ph.D. dissertation in Bioinformatics and Computational Biology at UNC Charlotte on November 10, 2026. I hold degrees in Biology and Education, and a Master's in Zoology.

My research covers terrestrial malacofauna worldwide, including taxonomy, phylogeny, and gastropod ecology. I work with natural history collections, museum genomics, Python programming, and modern evolutionary synthesis.

I actively participate in various societies, including the American Malacological Society, Sociedade Brasileira de Malacologia, Grupo Paranaense de Malacologia, and the Society for the Study of Evolution.

## My dissertation

"Museum genomics of _Triodopsis_ Rafinesque, 1819 (Stylommatophora: Polygyridae) land snails: closing the distance between bioinformatics and natural history collections." Advisor: Dr. Denis Jacob Machado. Committee: Dr. Daniel Janies, Dr. Robert Reid, Dr. Ann Loraine, and Dr. Adam Reitzel. Defended on November 10, 2026.

Four studies answer two questions. Can genome skimming of museum specimens resolve _Triodopsis_? Do the shell characters that define its species carry phylogenetic signal?

- I borrowed 123 specimens from six natural history collections and sequenced 69 of them by genome skimming. Mitochondrial genes came from 61 specimens, and nuclear markers came from 50.
- I built a parsimony tree of 1,081 specimen-level terminals from 20 markers and 22,897 aligned characters. Only 1 of 23 nominal _Triodopsis_ species is monophyletic, the _fallax_ group does not sort into species, and _T. tridentata_ does not split east and west of the Appalachians.
- I tested whether shell, habitat, and geographic characters taken from the taxonomic literature predict that tree. They do, and the signal is spread across many characters instead of resting on a few.
- I published a guide to requesting material from natural history collections.

GenBank has accepted 273 sequences from this work, and 147 more are submitted. The terminal registry, the literature table, the alignments, the trees, and the scripts are on [Zenodo](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23438660).
